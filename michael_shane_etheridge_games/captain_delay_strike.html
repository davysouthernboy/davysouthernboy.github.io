Captain Delay Strike

    Flight controls

    Move
    Arrow keys or WASD. On touch, drag your finger.
    Guns
    Hold Space. Guns fire on their own while you drag.
    Missile
    X or the Missile button. Homing, limited stock.
    Pause
    P.
    Esc
    Resets the game and returns to the title screen.
    Enter
    Starts from the title screen. Press it again to skip the cut scene.

    Soundtrack

    A built-in rock loop plays on launch. To fly to Highway to the Danger Zone, load your own copy of the song and it plays instead.
